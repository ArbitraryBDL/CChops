Our Mission

Connecticut Chops is being built to create a welcoming, disciplined, and inspiring environment where young musicians can grow in skill, confidence, and community. Through rehearsals, performances, and mentorship, we aim to build pride in the arts while making marching music accessible and joyful for all.

Built for the Greater Hartford Area

As a proposed organization rooted in the Greater Hartford Area, CChops is committed to serving local families and young people through accessible performance opportunities, leadership development, and positive community engagement. We believe marching music can strengthen both individual growth and civic pride.

Why CChops

CChops is about more than performance. It is about building confident young people through discipline, creativity, and genuine community connection.

Connecticut Chops is a proposed community marching arts organization based in Windsor, Connecticut. Its purpose is to provide musical education, performance opportunities, leadership development, and community engagement through marching music and the performing arts.
